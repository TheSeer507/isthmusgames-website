About

A studio on the isthmus

We are a tiny indie studio in Panama, the narrow strip of land that joins two continents and separates two oceans.

Our story

IsthmusGames was founded by Hasan Patel in Panama. The name comes from home: an isthmus is a narrow piece of land with water on both sides, connecting two larger places. Our emblem draws that as a little island shaped like a game controller.

Our first game, Neighborhood Kitchen, is a free cozy restaurant game that runs in the browser.

How we work

  • Free and fair. Neighborhood Kitchen is free to play, with no ads and no in-game purchases.
  • In the open. The game is in active development, new updates land almost every week, and every release has a public devlog.
  • With players. Comments on itch.io reach us directly, and Patreon supporters vote on what comes next.
  • Honest about tools. AI tools assisted development, including code, interface text and art iteration. The game does not generate content with AI while you play.
