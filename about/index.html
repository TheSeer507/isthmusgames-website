About

A studio on the isthmus

We're a tiny indie studio in Panama, the narrow strip of land that joins two continents and separates two oceans.

Our story

The name has two meanings. The first comes from home: an isthmus is a narrow piece of land with water on both sides, connecting two larger places. Panama is one, and our emblem draws that as a little island shaped like a game controller.

The second is about the games themselves. We want to be the bridge between the games we grew up with and the games of today. That means the charm and simple joy of classic games, made with modern tools, playable instantly in your browser and shaped in the open with our players.

Our first game, Neighborhood Kitchen, is a free cozy restaurant game where you grow a kitchen garden, cook dozens of recipes and turn your café into the favorite spot on the block.

How we work

  • Free and fair. Neighborhood Kitchen is free to play, with no ads and no in-game purchases.
  • In the open. The game is in active development, new updates land almost every week, and every release has a public devlog.
  • With players. Comments on itch.io reach us directly, and Patreon supporters vote on what comes next.
  • Honest about tools. AI tools assisted development, including code, interface text and art iteration. The game does not generate content with AI while you play.
