GAME
Neighborhood Kitchen
A free cozy restaurant game. Arrange your café, cook 62 recipes and get to know 13 neighbors, right in your browser.
Latest version: 0.16.0, released October 12, 2026
Status: In development playtest
Platform: Web browser on itch.io. Desktop recommended.
Price: Free, with no ads and no in-game purchases
Players: Single-player
Language: English
Engine: Phaser

FEATURES
- 62 recipes across starters, mains, drinks, pastries and desserts, each with five mastery levels
- Cuisines: Italian, Japanese, Mexican, Greek, American Diner and German
- 13 neighbors with preferences, requests and friendships
- Six NPC restaurants to visit from level 5
- Kitchen garden with ten crops
- Staff: cooks, servers and cleaners
- Decorate: furniture, floors and walls, room expansion, jukebox, ten matching dining sets
- Gatherings of six or eight, takeaway
- Saves kept locally in the browser. No cloud sync.
