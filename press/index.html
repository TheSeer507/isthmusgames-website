Description

Short: Neighborhood Kitchen is a free cozy restaurant game by IsthmusGames, an independent studio from Panama. Arrange your café, cook 62 recipes and get to know 13 neighbors, right in your browser.

Long: Start with a tiny corner café and make it yours. Arrange the tables, choose your dishes and watch your chibi team cook, serve and clean. Run an Italian trattoria, a Japanese kitchen, a Greek taverna or an all-day diner, grow ingredients in the kitchen garden, and turn first-time guests into neighborhood regulars. The game is free to play with no ads and no in-game purchases, and new updates land almost every week.
