What you do

Build the favorite spot on the block

Make the café yours

Create your owner and server, name your restaurant, then decorate, rotate furniture, change floors and walls, expand the room and pick a jukebox station.

Craft your own menu

Discover 62 recipes across starters, mains, drinks, pastries and desserts, and build each one to mastery level 5.

Run any kind of kitchen

Cook Italian, Japanese, Mexican, Greek, American Diner and German dishes, with matching dining sets for each.

Keep the kitchen moving

Hire cooks, servers and cleaners, upgrade stoves, ovens and counters, and assign cooks to their own stations.

Get to know 13 neighbors

Learn their preferences, plan their visits and follow requests for ingredient gifts and keepsakes.

Visit other restaurants

From level 5, explore six NPC restaurants as your character and help their owners with daily jobs.

Grow your own ingredients

Plant tomatoes, lettuce, potatoes, strawberries, mushrooms, tea, lemons, olives, apples and pumpkins. Crops grow while you are away and never wilt.

Host gatherings

Seat parties of six or eight, let happy diners stay for a coffee or dessert, or switch on takeaway when the kitchen can handle it.

Questions

Is Neighborhood Kitchen free?

Yes. It is free to play, with no ads and no in-game purchases. If you enjoy it, a tip on itch.io or a Patreon pledge helps keep the kitchen open.

Do I need to download anything?

No. The game runs in your browser on itch.io. Click Run game and your first restaurant opens.

Does it work on phones?

Desktop is recommended. On phones, play upright, because landscape panels can overlap. Safari on iPhone and iPad may forget progress when the page reloads.

Where are my saves kept?

In your browser. There is no cloud sync, so keep a backup with Menu, then Download save, before refreshing or updating. Private windows and clearing site data can lose saves.

Is there multiplayer?

No. Neighborhood Kitchen is single-player. Visits to real players and player-to-player trading are not included.

Is the game finished?

Not yet. It is an in-development playtest, prices and rewards are still being tuned, and new updates land almost every week.
